      How it's calculated

      Manila fare
      ₱50 for the first 2 km · +₱10/km up to 7 km · +₱15/km after
      Example: 10 km = 50 + 50 + 45 = ₱145

      Total fare = (subtotal × surge) + extra charges.

      Gas
      Liters = distance ÷ (km per liter)
      Gas cost = liters × (₱ per liter)

      Net kinita today
      Gross fares − total gas cost of today’s trips.
